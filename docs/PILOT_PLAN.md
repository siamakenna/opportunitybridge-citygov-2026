# OpportunityBridge · proposed 60-day pilot

**Proposal only: 25 adult learners, 25 verified opportunities, zero committed institutions, zero recruited learners, zero observed career outcomes.** A college career-services office and a municipal workforce/HR team must agree to named responsibilities before launch. The current demonstrator does not evaluate real vacancy eligibility.

## Decision and intervention

Test whether a requirement-linked evidence worksheet improves an advisor's ability to review a learner's next step. Recruit across current students, recent graduates and adult learners through open campus notices and accessible advising channels; avoid selection based on GPA or access to personal referrals. Offer the same curated opportunities, compensation for study participation, accommodations and human advising to everyone. Participation is voluntary and never affects services, grades or hiring.

The campus lead owns learner support, the municipal HR lead owns source accuracy, a maintainer owns the static app, and an evaluator owns assignment and scoring. Each host supplies a named backup. No organization or employer is implied to have agreed.

## Consent, access and privacy

Provide plain-language consent with purpose, voluntary tasks, expected 30-minute session, risks, withdrawal route and retention date. Use fictional cases for comparative tasks. A separate optional follow-up consent permits real career progress reporting; declining does not affect advising. Host staff retain any scheduling contacts in their existing approved system, separately from randomized study IDs. Keep evaluation sheets access-limited; delete linkage and raw feedback within 90 days of pilot close unless institutional policy requires otherwise. Publish aggregates only, suppress cells below five and avoid identifying free-text quotes. Follow the institution's research/ethics and student-record determination before recruitment; this document is not legal approval.

No mandatory account, payment or résumé upload. Offer printed worksheets, telephone/in-person assistance, accessible room scheduling and loaned devices. Reserve accommodation support; test with actual assistive technologies before calling the pilot accessible. Translations require review and additional budget. Do not rank by protected attributes or demographic proxies. Any optional demographic evaluation requires separate consent, host approval, safe aggregation and sufficient sample size. A cohort of 25 cannot establish subgroup parity or hiring effects.

## Four primary outcomes and decision thresholds

1. **Evidence correctness:** correct criterion/state/source judgments divided by all scored judgments, target ≥90%; separately report required-criterion errors and every false definite eligibility statement (target zero). Two blinded raters reconcile against full source snapshots.
2. **Actionable plan rate:** plans scoring ≥4/5 on source identity, requirement distinction, honest uncertainty, plausible artifact and dated next step, divided by all consenting task starters; target ≥80%. Report withdrawals and missing work in denominator and sensitivity analysis.
3. **Next-step follow-through:** consenting learners who report completing their chosen action within 14 days divided by all consenting follow-up learners, target ≥60%; separate self-report from advisor-confirmed actions. No automatic tracking pixels or collection of application IDs. Interviews/placements are exploratory descriptive outcomes, not causal success claims.
4. **Practical access and workload:** report task completion by device/accommodation route, blocking barriers, interaction counts and advisor review minutes; target zero unresolved blocking barriers and median review ≤15 minutes. Small groups remain qualitative, with missingness reported.

Thresholds are proposed decisions, not results or statistical power claims. Use the [benchmark protocol](BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md); freeze before recruitment. Report both manual and assisted denominators, task order, familiarity and actual measured time. Do not extrapolate scripted engine accuracy to people or employment outcomes.

## Risks and sustainability

Incorrect/stale rules: daily expiry check, twice-weekly human review and employer escalation; suspend affected records. Staff absence: backup steward and bounded 25-record inventory. Recruitment shortfall: report actual n, extend only with approval. Low uptake or confusing worksheets: revise after usability evidence; retain human/paper option. Privacy incident: stop collection, notify host owner, follow institutional response process. Limited partner capacity: do not launch rather than imply a commitment.

After day 60, continue only if the campus and municipal leads approve measured benefit, workload and source governance. A 25-record service estimates 2 content hours/week at $35 and 2 advising hours/week at $50, plus 2 maintainer hours/month at $60: about $857/month using 52/12 weeks, excluding new development and accommodations. Seek existing career-services/workforce budgets, not learner fees. Scale to a second campus only after a repeatable content audit and accessibility review. Never claim an endorsed referral or guaranteed job.
