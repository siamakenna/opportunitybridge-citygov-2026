Public career information can still contain difficult qualification language and substitutions. The account-free prototype makes selected interpretations inspectable while preserving missing facts and employer authority. It does not predict hiring or certify eligibility.

EQUITY STATEMENT

OpportunityBridge makes source explanations available without learner fees, login or professional referrals. This is an access hypothesis, not evidence that an employment gap has closed. Discovery categories describe opportunities; preferences and skill similarity never become certified eligibility or hiring scores.

Saving is optional. Fictional drafts require capture; shared-device risks, deletion and unverified imports are visible. Private resumes and applicant records do not belong in this public demo. The proposed pilot gives both manual and assisted conditions equivalent source information, human support, compensation and accommodations. Adult consent is voluntary, separate from services, grades and hiring. A prospective host must offer paper, telephone/in-person support and devices where needed; contact linkage stays in approved institutional systems.

Semantic controls, visible focus, text/list alternatives, reduced motion, narrow-screen reflow and readable exports support access. Actual keyboard/reflow checks are documented; actual 200% zoom and screen-reader use remain untested where native permissions/tools are unavailable. No WCAG certification is claimed. English-only content, cognitive load and unfamiliar-user usability need study. Negative reactions, incomplete tasks and barriers will be retained. A small pilot cannot establish subgroup parity or causal career effects.
