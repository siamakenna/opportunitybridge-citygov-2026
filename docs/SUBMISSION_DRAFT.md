# Existing Kenny! draft — v0.7 owner copy

SOLUTION DESCRIPTION

OpportunityBridge helps learners turn education and experience into an understandable public-service opportunity and a concrete advisor conversation. Qualification language can be unfamiliar, and an informal professional connection often supplies the explanation. Our working prototype makes that explanation inspectable: find a source, distinguish its review status, connect declared experience to proposed evidence, and keep unresolved questions visible. Students, recent graduates and career changers can use it without an account or fee.

Opportunity Pulse now provides an actual dated discovery feed from the RSS source advertised by Maryland’s official recruitment website. The initial response yielded 320 valid recruitment metadata records after strict identity filtering. These are not 320 individually reviewed or guaranteed open jobs. Search, category filters, review levels, sorting and tab-only saving help visitors explore opportunities. Publisher timestamps and observation dates remain distinct. Coverage is Maryland RSS plus our curated catalog, not a nationwide or personalized job search. USAJOBS integration is disabled because an owner-approved API key and registered email are unavailable.

Three evidence levels separate discovery metadata, full-source review and preparation-ready selected mappings. Discovery never assigns qualification rules. Daily GitHub observations can publish permitted metadata snapshots after tests; they cannot renew qualification-review dates or rewrite approved mappings. “What changed?” distinguishes publisher metadata differences, detected page changes, unavailable sources and confirmed clock-based expiry. A page hash difference never becomes an asserted requirement change. Runtime holds suppress positive interpretations when source observations need review. Published snapshot dates and a content hash make updates auditable, while the code revision remains explicit.

The reviewed twelve-record library preserves five specific Maryland recruitments, two research intakes, four ongoing pathways and one expired intake. Its original review dates are not advanced to make Pulse look recent. A featured MPT Media Literacy fellowship explains the student/recent-graduate route, sixteen-hour commitment, published compensation and college documents. A second administrative journey exposes six qualifying months or thirty accredited college credits. Fiscal accounting credits replace only one of two required experience years; uncertain equivalencies and stacking remain employer questions. The complete current announcement controls.

Evidence Passport is a portable planning document, not an official credential. Its progressive editor connects fictional or voluntary non-sensitive experience to a transferable skill, an actual source requirement or role task, a proposed artifact and a human-verification question. Visitors edit one stage at a time, revisit a compact overview and inspect an interactive map with a text equivalent. Deterministic completeness checks explain weak or missing connections without treating similarity as eligibility. Markdown, JSON and full-text printing retain source identity, review date, selected excerpts or official routes, unknowns, document status and catalog revision. Learner drafts remain local.

Career Stories retain five attributed historical examples. Visitors can inspect institutional deliverables versus first-person accounts, offers, contractor work and unknown outcomes, then add a clearly labeled comparable exercise to a Passport. That exercise is our recommendation, not the fellow’s artifact, current vacancy or endorsement. Advisor Studio assembles open questions, missing documents, deadline-aware targets and a human handoff. “Ready for human discussion” explicitly leaves eligibility unknown; an illustrative campus-advisor/municipal-HR route demonstrates an insufficient-information outcome. Nothing applies, messages or refers someone automatically.

A coherent navy, teal, violet, coral and ivory interface supplies shared navigation, visible source states, reduced-motion support and less dense editing. The captioned current walkthrough uses six genuine app screenshots and accurately labels its format. A proposed sixty-day campus/municipal pilot would test twenty-five adults and twenty-five reviewed sources. Matched-source tasks now probe discovery status, review age, weak evidence connections and handoff quality. Independent unfamiliar participants remain n=0. Working software and real exports exist today; career benefit, equitable access and faster advising remain hypotheses requiring independent people and willing institutions.

EQUITY STATEMENT

OpportunityBridge makes an explanation often supplied through professional networks available without charging learners, requiring accounts or collecting resumes. That is an access hypothesis, not proof that an employment gap has closed. Discovery categories describe sources rather than people. Related skills, preferred experience and field completeness never become certified eligibility or a hiring score.

The proposed pilot provides equivalent source packets, human advising, compensation and accommodations in manual and assisted conditions. Adult consent is voluntary and separate from services, grades and hiring. Paper worksheets, telephone/in-person support and devices should be available through a host. Contact/consent linkage stays separately in approved institutional systems; no private patient, student or job-seeker records belong in this public demo.

The interface uses labels, visible focus, progressive disclosure, a text equivalent for the evidence map, reduced-motion rules, narrow-screen reflow and readable exports. Tool-driven contrast and keyboard checks are documented. English-only content and absent independent assistive-technology testing remain limits; no WCAG certification is claimed. We would preserve skipped work, unsupported conclusions, negative feedback, access barriers and exact denominators. A small pilot cannot establish subgroup parity or causal hiring effects. Historical fellows are never presented as product users or endorsers.

PILOT AND MEASURES

Proposed: sixty days, twenty-five consenting adults and twenty-five double-reviewed opportunities. Six staff functions cover campus advising, municipal source adjudication, content stewardship, maintenance, evaluation and accessibility. The existing manager PDF, interactive partner route, checklists, anonymous worksheet and blank two-rater tracking CSV remain available. No institution, funds or labor are committed.

Illustrative cost: 112 staff hours/$5,740 plus $375 compensation and $125 materials/access reserve; $6,240 economic total. Cash can be $500 only if all labor is contributed. Host approval must validate rates, capacity, overhead and accommodations. Minimize data; delete contact linkage/raw feedback within ninety days unless approved policy requires otherwise.

Targets, not results: at least 90% criterion/source correctness and zero false definite eligibility claims; at least 80% actionable plans scoring 4/5; at least 60% reported fourteen-day follow-through, separating self-report from advisor confirmation; zero unresolved blocking access barriers and median advisor review at most 15 measured minutes.

EVIDENCE, MEDIA AND DISCLOSURE

Executed checks and failures are linked in the release report. Independent n=0; no causal speed or hiring gain. Source-backed AI-assisted reviews are not independent HR validation. Daily metadata updates do not replace explicit source review near submission. The unavailable v0.3 Impact Lab was not integrated and its reported 17 tests are not claimed. Internal ten-criterion mock assessment remains 87/100; additional features do not substitute for independent impact, accessibility or partner evidence.

The 150-second v0.7 walkthrough is six authentic local working-app captures assembled as a captioned screenshot sequence, not continuous footage. It includes a genuinely downloaded Passport example and preserves capture-time source dates. English captions/transcript are provided; the earlier video is retained. No narration, fabricated participants or testimonials. The 5–7-minute finalist pitch and Q&A are prepared.

ChatGPT supplied the starter; Codex significantly assisted research, code, design, deterministic workflows, evaluation, documents, testing, media and publication. Owner review remains necessary. MIT source; static browser runtime, system fonts, public RSS, Node/Python tests and GitHub Pages. No paid API or live language-model agent. Prior-project methods were adapted from the brief without copying private code, data or branding. Official publishers retain their source materials.
