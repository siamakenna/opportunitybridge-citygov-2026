# Existing Kenny! CityGov draft — v0.8 copy

SOLUTION DESCRIPTION

OpportunityBridge: Career Quest helps learners turn unfamiliar public-service opportunities into a small learning action, a source-linked evidence plan and a concrete human conversation. Education, classroom work and mentoring do not automatically translate into understandable employer requirements. The prototype makes that translation inspectable without an account or fee, while preserving missing information and employer authority.

Three playable quests are the centerpiece. Source Detective uses a frozen Maryland Administrative Specialist I source. Learners must identify the thirty-accredited-credit substitution for six administrative months, select official evidence, recognize missing applicant facts and reject a hiring guarantee. All five checks must pass. The version remains clearly historical if the announcement changes or closes; learning completion never certifies present eligibility.

Build an Accessibility Artifact supplies a fictional library webpage with five concrete problems. Learners change meaningful map text, text contrast, heading structure, link purpose and a form label. The actual preview changes, feedback explains each decision, and a repaired self-contained HTML demo and JSON decision log can be downloaded. Only those predefined conditions are evaluated. No WCAG certification or completed professional work is implied.

Career Evidence Builder connects a fictional experience to a transferable skill, a valid reviewed MPT role duty or condition, a proposed artifact and a human-verification question. Empty stages, invalid source associations and selected unsupported hiring claims cannot complete it. Successful transfer opens all five editable Passport fields. Guided controls, previous/next navigation, samples, missing-field feedback, previews and resets reduce the earlier wizard friction. JSON and Markdown retain source identity, review date, quest version, frozen snapshot and unknowns.

The Quest Board and freely navigable journey map connect Explore, Try, Build and Launch. A deterministic ledger awards learning progress once per quest/version/session, retaining actual checks and completion time. Repeated clicks or replays cannot farm rewards. Tokens describe in-app learning actions, never hiring worthiness or employer-recognized credentials. Plain learning view hides points and decoration; visitors can skip games and use conventional career tools directly. All drafts and progress remain in memory unless downloaded.

Supporting discovery is deliberately bounded. Maryland’s advertised RSS is the only connected feed. Structured classifications correct the INTERNAL/intern false positive and distinguish publisher-stated types, reviewed types, inferred candidates and unknowns. Graduate alone never means entry-level; contractual never means short-term. Multi-select categories, goals, pay/cost, arrangement, duration, stage and explicit unknown filters combine with normalized search. Reset restores the original catalog. Interests only reorder results, without demographic inference or eligibility prediction.

Twelve curated reviewed/pathway/history records retain their original qualification-review dates. Three checked learning resources add OpenLearn and Forage training; three directory routes support apprenticeship, service and micro-project exploration. Courses are not jobs. No individual short paid project could be verified, so that filter returns an honest empty state with an outbound Parker Dewey directory. USAJOBS API remains disabled pending owner-controlled credentials. Daily observations publish permitted metadata and hold uncertain sources without approving qualification mappings.

Five attributed historical cases can add proposed comparable exercises, with original provenance and unknown career outcomes preserved. Advisor Studio prepares unresolved questions, documents and deadline-aware targets for a human handoff. Nothing applies or messages automatically. Repeated frozen browser trials, actual downloaded files and timing receipts are documented; these are software checks, not independent usability findings. Independent unfamiliar participants remain n=0. A proposed sixty-day campus/municipal pilot would test twenty-five adults and twenty-five reviewed sources. Career benefit, equitable access and return engagement remain hypotheses.

EQUITY STATEMENT

OpportunityBridge makes an explanation often supplied through professional networks available without charging learners, requiring accounts or collecting resumes. That is an access hypothesis, not proof that an employment gap has closed. Discovery categories describe sources rather than people. Related skills, preferred experience and field completeness never become certified eligibility or a hiring score.

The proposed pilot provides equivalent source packets, human advising, compensation and accommodations in manual and assisted conditions. Adult consent is voluntary and separate from services, grades and hiring. Paper worksheets, telephone/in-person support and devices should be available through a host. Contact/consent linkage stays separately in approved institutional systems; no private patient, student or job-seeker records belong in this public demo.

The interface uses labels, visible focus, progressive disclosure, a text equivalent for the evidence map, reduced-motion rules, narrow-screen reflow and readable exports. Tool-driven contrast and keyboard checks are documented. English-only content and absent independent assistive-technology testing remain limits; no WCAG certification is claimed. We would preserve skipped work, unsupported conclusions, negative feedback, access barriers and exact denominators. A small pilot cannot establish subgroup parity or causal hiring effects. Historical fellows are never presented as product users or endorsers.

PILOT AND MEASURES

Proposed: sixty days, twenty-five consenting adults and twenty-five double-reviewed opportunities. Six staff functions cover campus advising, municipal source adjudication, content stewardship, maintenance, evaluation and accessibility. The existing manager PDF, interactive partner route, checklists, anonymous worksheet and blank two-rater tracking CSV remain available. No institution, funds or labor are committed.

Illustrative cost: 112 staff hours/$5,740 plus $375 compensation and $125 materials/access reserve; $6,240 economic total. Cash can be $500 only if all labor is contributed. Host approval must validate rates, capacity, overhead and accommodations. Minimize data; delete contact linkage/raw feedback within ninety days unless approved policy requires otherwise.

Targets, not results: at least 90% criterion/source correctness and zero false definite eligibility claims; at least 80% actionable plans scoring 4/5; at least 60% reported fourteen-day follow-through, separating self-report from advisor confirmation; zero unresolved blocking access barriers and median advisor review at most 15 measured minutes.

EVIDENCE, MEDIA AND DISCLOSURE

Executed checks and failures are linked in the release report. Independent n=0; no causal speed or hiring gain. Source-backed AI-assisted reviews are not independent HR validation. Daily metadata updates do not replace explicit source review near submission. The unavailable v0.3 Impact Lab was not integrated and its reported 17 tests are not claimed. Internal ten-criterion mock assessment remains 87/100; the owner-reported external automated estimate was approximately 85/100; additional features do not substitute for independent impact, accessibility or partner evidence.

The 180-second v0.8 walkthrough is seven authentic local working-app captures assembled as a captioned screenshot sequence, not continuous footage. It includes three playable quests and a genuinely downloaded Passport example and preserves capture-time source dates. English captions/transcript are provided; the earlier video is retained. No narration, fabricated participants or testimonials. The 5–7-minute finalist pitch and Q&A are prepared.

ChatGPT supplied the starter; Codex significantly assisted research, code, design, deterministic workflows, evaluation, documents, testing, media and publication. Owner review remains necessary. MIT source; static browser runtime, system fonts, public RSS, Node/Python tests and GitHub Pages. No paid API or live language-model agent. Prior-project methods were adapted from the brief without copying private code, data or branding. Official publishers retain their source materials.
