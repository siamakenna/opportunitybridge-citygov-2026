## Content stewardship

The proposed campus content steward checks links before every advising session and twice weekly during a pilot. Record reviewer, UTC observation, employer, recruitment ID, full requirements including substitutions, location, compensation, deadline/timezone, source URL and change notes. A second reviewer must approve a real rule mapping before the product may evaluate it. Present release contains no real-rule mappings.

Mark withdrawn records immediately; archive passed deadlines and retain the citation with its historical observation label. Do not silently extend dates. Reverify at least 48 hours before submission. Source excerpts are brief attributed citations, not copied vacancy databases; no permission to redistribute entire postings is asserted. Applicant records and medical/financial details must never be copied into the site. Correct source errors through the official source; do not rely on potentially mistyped contact details.
