PROBLEM & SOLUTION

A learner can have useful classroom, research or community experience and still struggle to turn it into a credible public-service application. Job titles vary, qualification language is unfamiliar, and an informal connection often supplies the explanation. OpportunityBridge makes that explanation inspectable: start with an official opportunity, understand selected requirements, prepare evidence and bring a concrete question to a human advisor.

The working app leads with real opportunities. Its October 26 inventory distinguishes four specific Maryland recruitments, two dated federal research intakes, four ongoing career/search pathways and one expired fellowship intake. These are eleven different records, not eleven open jobs. Two newly inspected Maryland Public Television student fellow recruitments are open/continuous; NIH Summer Internship Program and DOE SULI Summer 2027 have announced deadlines beyond November 6. These four are deadline-compatible options, not a guarantee of future availability. The administrative and fiscal Maryland vacancies close November 3 and 5. Every record retains its official source, recruitment identity when applicable, observed date, application destination, selected conditions, location, compensation, materials and limits.

Source freshness is operational. An actual retrieval script compares normalized announcement content, recruitment identity, excerpts and withdrawal signals and emits a review queue. It cannot renew dates or authorize qualification rules. An explicit source-backed review must record actual inspection time, findings, source hash and the exact selected mapping snapshot. Unapproved mapping changes return unknown. Six records were re-inspected October 26; other dates were preserved. Automated retrieval failures remain reported, including NIH SIP, which was separately inspected through the official web reader. This is source-backed AI-assisted review, not independent HR approval.

After 48 hours, sources become previously observed and positive selected-condition interpretations are suppressed. Passed deadlines leave default results but retain history. Coding it Forward remains expired by its stated October 25 cutoff even if its page still invites applications. The static app cannot detect every early withdrawal; the complete current employer announcement always controls. A source steward must recheck near November 4–5 rather than automatically advancing timestamps.

Qualification discoverability now starts on each vacancy card. The Maryland administrative card names six qualifying administrative months or 30 accredited college credits, and the military route's published conditions. A direct 30-credit-route button focuses the actual numeric input. The fiscal card makes clear that six accounting credits replace only one year of the required two years; vocational/military routes need review and stacking is not assumed. No applicant is certified eligible.

Selecting a record opens the requirement-to-evidence workspace. Visitors use fictional declarations, inspect exact source fragments and contextual summaries, distinguish mandatory conditions from preferences, and retain three states: declared evidence appears consistent, possible conflict or insufficient information. Sensitive conditions and ambiguous equivalencies remain employer questions. Visitors draft a fictional evidence note and download a portfolio plan or application checklist/advisor handoff containing real recruitment identity, edited facts, unresolved questions, materials and a 14-day action plan bounded by the employer's actual deadline. Official applications remain primary; optional search channels do not imply a partnership.

The historical gallery distinguishes NIH institutional project reports from former Civic Innovation Corps participants' own published accounts. It separates documented deliverables, self-reported offers and contractor work from unknown outcomes. Suggested portfolio exercises are ours. These people are not OpportunityBridge users or endorsers, and their experiences do not establish our placement outcomes.

The original fictional engine remains in clearly labeled Practice Mode with its baseline tests. The app is free, static and account-free, with no resume upload, runtime model or automatic application. Notes and shortlists stay in memory and clear on reload. Downloads stay on the device; hosting logs and external-site policies are disclosed.

EVALUATION AND HONEST LIMITS

A new ready-to-run guided instrument uses two genuine reviewed Maryland sources, equal starting packets and explicit manual/assisted conditions. Four sequences rotate case and order. It records consent/rehearsal labels, actual task intervals, five response fields per task, optional observer-counted actions, missing work and unfavorable/accessibility reflections. Local JSON never automatically becomes verified participant evidence. Two raters would score source accuracy, condition distinctions, honest uncertainty, plausible work samples and feasible next actions. The host must independently check full sources/gold labels before recruitment and replace expired cases. Equal difficulty and causal speed gains are unvalidated.

Independent unfamiliar participants remain n=0. The only downloaded rehearsal was implementer/software QA; its automated intervals are not learner performance. The v0.3 Impact Lab archive was unavailable, so it was not integrated and its reported 17 tests are not claimed. Actual software checks: 67 Node tests; 23 original real-workflow, 15 Practice Mode and nine new guided-evaluation browser assertions; eight frozen real-source fixture checks and twelve retained fictional judgments. These measure software behavior, not career impact.

EQUITY STATEMENT

OpportunityBridge addresses an information barrier often navigated through informal professional networks: interpreting requirements, recognizing relevant experience and deciding what to ask an advisor. Making those steps visible without charging learners, requiring accounts or collecting resumes is a design hypothesis, not evidence that an employment gap has closed.

Unknown facts remain unknown. Related skills do not certify eligibility and preferences do not replace mandatory experience. The app does not rank people by protected characteristics or demographic proxies. Historical fellows illustrate work in other programs, never product testimonials or placement promises.

The proposed pilot offers equivalent source information, human advising, compensation and accommodations in both conditions. Printed worksheets, telephone/in-person support and loaned devices provide alternatives. Consent is voluntary and separate from services; private participant records never enter the public app. Labeled controls, visible focus, responsive layouts, reduced motion and printable checklists support access. English-only content and no independent screen-reader or accessibility audit remain limits. Report errors, missing data, withdrawals, assistance, unfavorable findings and workload alongside completion. A small pilot cannot establish subgroup parity or causal hiring effects.

PILOT AND COST

The new two-page manager packet proposes 60 days, 25 consenting adult learners and 25 double-reviewed opportunities. College career services recruits and advises; a city workforce/HR reviewer adjudicates full source conditions; a steward, maintainer, evaluator and accessibility reviewer own updates, defects, blinded scoring and alternatives. No organization or funding is committed. Illustrative labor: 112 hours/$5,740; compensation $375; materials/access reserve $125; total economic cost $6,240. Direct cash can be $500 only if all staff labor is contributed. Rates, capacity and accommodations require host confirmation. Keep contact linkage separate from pseudonymous tasks; delete linkage/raw feedback within 90 days unless approved policy requires otherwise.

PROPOSED OUTCOMES: At least 90% criterion/source correctness and zero false definite eligibility statements; at least 80% actionable plans scoring 4/5; at least 60% reported 14-day follow-through, separating self-report from advisor confirmation; zero unresolved blocking access barriers and median advisor review at most 15 measured minutes. These are targets, not results. Internal ten-criterion mock review is 85/100, not an official rating; independent impact, source adjudication, access review and partner commitment remain deductions.
