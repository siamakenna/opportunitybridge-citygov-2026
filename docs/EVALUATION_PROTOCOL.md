## Recruit and consent

Invite one to three unfamiliar adults or career advisors through an authorized host. No invitations have been sent, no participants enrolled and no partner committed. Explain that the software is AI-assisted, participation is voluntary, services/grades/hiring are unaffected, and they can skip, stop or withdraw before anonymized aggregation. Explain the host's withdrawal contact, approved retention and any compensation before consent. Offer paper/telephone assistance, devices, breaks and actual assistive technology. No recording by default. Never enter real student, patient, education or job-seeker records.

Use P01-style codes. Contact/consent linkage stays in the host's approved system, separate from task JSON. Delete linkage and raw feedback within 90 days unless approved policy requires otherwise. The public app sends nothing; downloaded files remain on the device and must be shared deliberately. Clear the local session after export. Hosting and external application sites have their own logs/policies.

## Freeze and counterbalance

Allocate sequences 1–4 cyclically before observing a participant: manual A/assisted B, assisted A/manual B, manual B/assisted A, assisted B/manual A. Record assignment and any deviations. Every case supplies identical fictional facts, exact selected source excerpts, summaries and official destination in both conditions. Manual permits ordinary source navigation, browser find and blank notes; assisted additionally permits OpportunityBridge. Both allow equivalent human accessibility assistance. Do not teach the answer in one condition. A and B are different cases; equal difficulty has not been validated. Small n, order and learning effects preclude causal speed claims.

The admin case has zero administrative months plus 30 accredited credits. Its selected experience route may appear consistent; all other conditions remain unknown. The fiscal case has 12 qualifying months plus six accounting credits. The credits replace only one year; no stacking or overall eligibility is inferred. The independent municipal reviewer must verify all source conditions and acceptable evidence before these become study gold labels. These are current source-backed author fixtures, not independent gold.

## Observe the complete task

Allow 15–20 minutes total. Start each task only when the participant is ready. The timer measures actual Begin-to-Finish wall time using a monotonic browser clock, including external source reading, interruptions and app use. Record pauses/help as deviations; do not subtract them silently. An optional observer count is actual external actions across tabs; leave unknown when not observed. The instrument does not monitor external browsing. A self-declared participant label never changes the project's verified n.

Each of two tasks requests five outputs: official source/application route; required/preferred/substitution understanding; unknowns; plausible portfolio artifact plus advisor question; dated next action. Missing answers are retained and counted out of five. Download the local JSON after reflection. Collect unfavorable findings and accessibility/comprehension problems verbatim only with consent. No correctness, independent participant count or speed advantage is automatically awarded.

## Score and report

Two independent raters, blinded to condition if practical, score each handoff 0/1 on five criteria: correct source and live deadline; correct required/preferred/substitution distinctions; explicit uncertainty without eligibility certification; plausible non-fabricated work sample; feasible next action/advisor question. Preserve both raw scores, disagreement and adjudication separately. Missing rubric remains null. Report each item denominator, full task starters/completers, withdrawals, missing fields and assistance. Time summaries must name cases, order and timing conditions; do not combine implementer rehearsals with unfamiliar participants. If n is small, publish individual de-identified counts/ranges only with consent, not population or subgroup claims.

Current evidence: implementer/software rehearsal only. Independent participants n=0; no observed learner benefit, causal efficiency gain, hiring outcome or validated advisor quality.
