## Actual implemented product

The three playable mini-quests are the centerpiece. Source Detective requires a source-backed 30-credit/six-month substitution, appropriate evidence, recognition of missing applicant facts and rejection of a hiring guarantee. It always identifies its frozen historical recruitment/version, regardless of current vacancy status. The accessibility quest changes a fictional library page through five objective repairs and produces a real self-contained HTML demo and JSON log. Career Evidence Builder requires populated experience, an allowed skill, a valid real MPT source association, a proposed artifact and a meaningful human question. All provide immediate explanatory feedback, retry/reset, a downloadable local artifact and a source-bound Passport route.

The Quest Board and six freely accessible journey destinations support Explore → Try → Build → Launch. A versioned in-memory ledger stores actual checks, source snapshot and timestamp once per quest/version, with 30/40/40 learning XP (maximum 110). Replays and clicks do not farm rewards. Tokens name completed learning actions only. Plain learning view hides XP/decoration and turns the cards into a list; real opportunities and conventional advising tools remain accessible without games. No persistence, ranking, streaks, scarcity, countdowns, demographic inference or employment match score.

The Student/Fellowship substring bug is corrected as a release blocker. INTERNAL AUDITOR II and INTERNAL INVESTIGATION DETECTIVE SERGEANT are not internships; graduate-profession titles do not become entry-level; contractual does not become a short contract. Structured types retain publisher-stated, source-reviewed, inferred-candidate or unknown provenance independently. MPT reviewed fellows retain fellowship/part-time classifications; MPT Spring Internship title metadata does not invent pay or eligibility. Both publication workflows run the explicit classification gate before publication, in addition to the full suite.

Discovery has multi-select category chips, goals, pay/cost, arrangement, stage, duration, weekly commitment, field/location, recent observation, evidence level, saved items and deliberate unknown controls. Chips combine with OR; other filters combine with AND. Normalized accent/punctuation/case search has no fuzzy occupational matches. Interests reorder only; Reset all filters restores original IDs/order and Reset whole session also clears all learner/quest state.
