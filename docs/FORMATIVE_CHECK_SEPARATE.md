# Separate formative usability check

Prepared protocol only. Zero sessions executed. This is a formative product review and does not amend or replace the frozen kit 2.0.1/shortform 2.0, its assignments, facilitator key, or original study records. Follow any applicable host approval requirements before recruitment or data collection. Use consenting adults, fictional input and existing free tools. Send invitations only with owner authorization.

## Setup

Recruit available adult learners unfamiliar with this app. Three is a practical discovery exercise, not a power calculation. Record how they were recruited and whether they know the owner. Include negative or incomplete sessions. Participation is optional, with no effect on advising or access. Seek one career-services practitioner to review exports. Two independent raters are preferred for artifact scoring. If one person rates, state that explicitly. Author ratings are internal.

Record a random session code, actual date, candidate SHA, task, route, device/browser and whether any assistance occurred. Do not record names, demographic details, contact records or private applications in app inputs or the public CSV. Retain consent status separately from task answers. Obtain separate permission for quotations or screenshots. Default to notes without recording. Give participants a stop/withdrawal route and state a local retention date before starting. Follow applicable institutional policy.

## Facilitator introduction

“We are checking whether this prototype helps people prepare a useful question and plan for an advisor. The app is being tested. You can stop or skip any question. Use the fictional scenario provided and avoid personal information. I will note where the interface helps or becomes confusing. May I record anonymous written notes for this product review?”

If no consent, do not collect observations. If consent is withdrawn, follow the agreed deletion procedure. Stop if the participant would need to enter private information to continue.

## Task

Use the recovered candidate's public link only after it is approved and published, or its owner-provided local preview with access recorded. Say “Use the fictional HTML learner example to prepare something you could take to an advisor. Find what the employer asks for, download the plan, and tell me what you would do next.” Let the participant choose the visible interface. Avoid directing clicks. Record assistance verbatim if required.

Assign a different available participant to the spreadsheet route if that route is present. Record task assignment rather than treating different routes as equivalent cases. If a source is closed or stale, safe rechecking or choosing an alternative is an appropriate result. Do not coach participants into a desired answer.

After the task ask these neutral questions.

1. In your own words, what could this plan help you do?
2. Which part came from the employer, and which part was proposed for practice?
3. What is still unanswered about this fictional learner?
4. What would you do first, and why?
5. What would you change or remove?

Record answers before comparing them with source and export evidence. A first action should agree with the actual source state. Stale or held sources need a current announcement check. A correct explanation should identify a source-grounded duty, a proposed sample and a remaining qualification or employer question. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a binary score for ambiguous answers.

## Reporting

Report recruited, consented, started, completed, withdrew and missing counts separately. State how many completed independently and how many needed help. Give counts with denominators. Define completion as a downloaded plan plus answers to the source/proposal/unknown/next-action questions. A partial task remains partial even if the participant likes the app.

Optional elapsed time is descriptive and needs actual start/stop times and interruption notes. This single-arm exercise establishes no speed advantage. No causal employment benefit, generalizable success rate, subgroup equity conclusion or validated readiness score follows from this exercise.

Example report TEMPLATE with empty fields.

“Of __ consenting adults who started, __ downloaded a plan independently and __ with assistance. __ identified the employer duty, proposed sample and unresolved question. The observed blockers were __. After correction, __ retested the affected step. These convenience observations assess usability, with no comparison group or measured hiring effect.”

## Advisor review

Give the actual export and its official source to the reviewer. Do not provide participant names or a desired score. Record relevant role, relationship to owner and whether review is independent. For each item mark yes, no, or unclear and quote the reason.

- Correct source and duty identity.
- An understandable connection between fictional classroom work and the duty.
- Honest distinction between proposed and completed evidence.
- A feasible next action consistent with source freshness/closure.
- An unresolved question that could support an advising conversation.

Ask “Could this help structure an appointment? What would you still need before using it?” Record any willingness statement in its exact scope. A personal offer to review another plan is not an institutional pilot commitment. Retain each rater's initial assessment and disagreements. Keep any institutional launch gates in force.

## Source-maintenance observation

A competent reviewer chooses one representative full official source. Record start/stop, source URL and retrieval result, source age/status, actual review steps, ambiguity and any follow-up required. Do not clear a hold without sufficient review. An implementer's observation is internally measured work, not independent source adjudication. Record failed attempts as workload. Compare the actual tasks with the modeled tasks before comparing times.

The existing Stage 1 source-review assumption is 0.5 hour per source per reviewer. The 25-source service models two content hours weekly, two advising hours weekly and two maintenance hours monthly. Use observed workload to flag an assumption for revision. One task or fastest run does not validate a recurring cost forecast. Capacity needs a named owner and real availability.

## Accessibility observation

On the exact candidate, test the full critical flow with keyboard only, actual browser 200% zoom and an available real screen reader. Record OS/browser/AT/version, tested route and display size, task steps, barriers and remediation. Include labels, focus after plan generation, announcements, next-action order and usable export controls. Rendering a DOM tree is not screen-reader operation. Tests on v0.9.3 do not certify the unpublished candidate. Report blocked tests as blocked and avoid WCAG conformance claims.
