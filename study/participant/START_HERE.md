# Help us make career exploration easier

Two activities, about 15 minutes. Use fictional examples, not personal job records. We are testing the website, not you. Adults 18+ only; participation is voluntary and stopping affects no services or opportunities. No account, installation, recording or automatic upload.

1. Discuss consent, withdrawal, sharing and retention with your organizer. They assign Session A, B, C or D; do not choose your own condition.
2. Open the assigned SESSION_*.html in a modern browser. Works as a local file or hosted page. You can use paper TASK_CARDS.md or speak while a facilitator records responses.
3. Get ready for two minutes, spend at most five minutes on each activity, then use three minutes for optional feedback, saving and debrief. Three brief answers per activity; a full game or Passport is not required.
4. Source-only means use the employer announcement and notes, without OpportunityBridge. Assisted means the same source plus the app. The facts and questions are identical for a case in either condition. If a source is unavailable, changed or expired, say so; it is not automatically your mistake. Do not apply or register during this study.
5. Use Save My Responses whenever you wish, even with blank or partial work. It generates a .json file on your device. You do not need to open/edit it; a readable .txt alternative is available. A request to the browser is not proof a download succeeded. Check your Downloads folder; nothing is sent to the organizer automatically.
6. Stop/withdraw at any point. Your local export records withdrawal, not successful participation. Share only by choice under your organizer's policy. No autosave: closing the page loses tab-only entries.

Do not enter names, emails, passwords, IDs, health information, transcripts, resumes or actual applications. Common identifier/credential patterns are blocked before export, but this cannot detect every private detail. The official source can change and this exercise never certifies eligibility, accessibility compliance, a placement or an employer credential.
