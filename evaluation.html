OpportunityBridgeJudge’s guide

READY-TO-RUN INSTRUMENT · INDEPENDENT n=0

Test the real source-to-advisor journey.

A host can use this 15-20-minute instrument with one to three unfamiliar consenting adults. Choose two source-grounded exercises have the same starting information in manual and assisted conditions. The four sequences rotate both case and order within the chosen pair. They are comparable exercises, not validated equal-difficulty tasks.

Use fictional facts only. No names, contacts, private education records or real job-seeker histories. Nothing is uploaded. Local exports are self-declared observations; they do not update the project’s independent participant count. Host source/gold review and consent are required before study use.

Session setup and consent

Participation is voluntary and unrelated to services, grades or hiring. You may skip, stop or clear your work. The host must explain the purpose, retention, withdrawal contact and accommodations before requesting consent. Paper/telephone assistance is equally available. Downloads remain on your device; share only by choice.

After setup, the host gives the same source packet and permits normal browser find/notes in both conditions. Independent results remain unverified until reviewed with consent.
