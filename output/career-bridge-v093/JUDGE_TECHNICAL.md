## Product capability versus potential impact

The receipts and inspected code support a source-linked plan, a bounded MPT duty connection, conservative alternative paths and actual portable artifacts. They do not establish that a learner understands the plan, saves time, creates better work, gains an interview or experiences more equitable access. Software assertions are useful engineering evidence without being substitutes for those observations.

The reported public deployment and CI results strengthen release confidence. This perspective did not reperform those browser/test runs. The 12/12 source-assisted cases and 28/28 frozen authored challenge are reproducible authored evaluations; the author knew the cases, normal RSS lacks those descriptions, and independent adjudicators remain zero. Historical 6/12 classification is retained rather than erased.

## Competitive arguments

**Strongest:** An inspectable account-free preparation workflow makes the boundary between employer facts, learner declarations, proposed work and unresolved decisions visible in the export. Reliability and source restraint are more defensible differentiators than headline authored benchmark accuracy.

**Weakest:** Claiming superior matching, broad automated skill translation, production classification accuracy, employer-recognized credentials or better career outcomes than source-only/manual/chatbot/Handshake/Forage workflows. None is established by this packet.

## Technical/source-integrity observations

- bridge-core.js directly special-cases mpt-media/software and emits generic evidence/exploration proposals elsewhere. This is a reliable bounded prototype, not general semantic inference.
- classification-core.js uses a small explicit-language parser and an inspected registry. Source-binding guards help ingestion integrity but do not authenticate arbitrary third-party JSON; this is a trust-boundary limitation, not a demonstrated exploit.
- Bridge UI text uses textContent; downloads use local Blob URLs and revoke them. These are positive implementation choices, not sufficient evidence for complete security.
- The sample plan catalogRevision is local, consistent with the documented capture artifact. It is not evidence that the public deployment has a revision mismatch.

## Remaining weaknesses

- Only the MPT software path has the inspected dedicated direct duty mapping; additional routes need authored source review and mapping.
- Normal RSS lacks the rich synthetic descriptions behind 12/12 and 28/28; two metadata observations do not establish broad production coverage.
- Historical 6/12 classification remains a relevant adverse baseline; there is no independent external gold-set evaluation.
- All source qualification reviews and holds remain unrenewed; no availability or applicant eligibility determination follows.
- No host/advisor/funding commitment and no observed staff throughput or participant support burden.
- Actual 200% zoom, screen-reader operation, human usefulness and subgroup access outcomes are untested.
- Security observations are scoped to permitted modules; no full dependency, deployment, import/export or penetration audit performed.
- Each action currently reuses the same selected duty/requirement in its sourceEvidence field, which is weaker than action-specific document/condition citations.

The most valuable next evidence would be a properly resourced five-person feasibility run with independent plan ratings, actual assistive-technology checks and observed review/support workload, followed by externally adjudicated real-feed cases. These would resolve the largest deductions without assuming placement outcomes are required for a prototype entry.
