## Competitive case

**Strongest:** A learner can leave a public-source search with a concrete, portable preparation plan that preserves the boundary between an actual employer duty, a fictional skill declaration, a proposed artifact and an unresolved eligibility decision. That is a credible workforce-advising aid worth a bounded feasibility pilot.

**Weakest:** The case that it produces better career or equity outcomes, or can operate more cheaply at scale than existing advising and manual source review, is currently hypothetical. There is no human comparative advantage, employer recognition, recurring funding or operating commitment. The equivalent-task comparison expressly allows equally good manual preparation and does not claim a timed win: [docs/COMPARATIVE_VALUE.md](</Users/makennarodriguez/Documents/ChatGPT/hackthon projs/opportunitybridge_citygov_v0_1/docs/COMPARATIVE_VALUE.md>).

## Remaining weaknesses and evidence limits

- One owner-reported informal reviewer is not a verified timed participant study; zero verified timed human sessions and zero independent benchmark adjudicators remain.
- 12/12 on source-assisted known authored cases and 28/28 on a frozen authored challenge are bounded engineering results. The challenge was visible to its author, descriptive inputs are richer than ordinary RSS, and no production-feed accuracy or human efficacy follows.
- Public deployment/build, 233 asset byte checks, 209 preserved assertions and 13 new Bridge workflows are supplied release context; I did not independently rerun them. They do not represent users or placements.
- Source reviews/holds were not renewed by Employment Type metadata retrieval. The sample retains a source hold; applicant eligibility and employer acceptance remain unknown.
- Actual 200% zoom and screen reader are UNTESTED. No WCAG certification or equitable usability outcome is established.
- No committed host, advisor or funding; planned labor rates, review times and support demand are assumptions.

The most consequential next evidence would be five genuinely consented learner sessions, two independent plan ratings with disagreements retained, accessible completion with the needed accommodations, refreshed double-reviewed sources, named operational owners and actual staff-time receipts. These would support a stage-two decision; they would still not establish placement impact.
