## Strongest competitive case

- A concrete skill-to-reviewed-public-employer-duty connection culminates in a real export, rather than a generic statement of career ambition.
- Visible separation of source facts, learner declarations, proposed exercises and unresolved employer decisions makes uncertainty inspectable.
- The working alternative, unsupported-skill and expired-source paths and honest adverse evidence support technical credibility.
- A small, explicitly costed and gated first stage makes the next empirical step reviewable.

## Weakest competitive case

- Authored classifier scores or browser assertions imply career impact, production accuracy or superiority; they do not.
- The account-free integration is uniquely better than a capable advisor, grounded chatbot or established platform; no equivalent human or competitor test supports that.
- Learners lacking networks gain access merely because an advisor question is exported; advisor availability and response are unresolved.
- Low hosting cost establishes sustainable delivery; the dominant source-review and support labor is unvalidated and unfunded.

## Demonstration versus impact

The inspected [sample plan](SAMPLE_CAREER_BRIDGE_PLAN.md) concretely links fictional HTML learning to the MPT duty, names unknown conditions and exports three planned actions. [Local acceptance](LOCAL_BRIDGE_ACCEPTANCE.json) supports the functional paths. The supplied release report adds 233 matching public assets, 209 preserved assertions and 13 passing public Bridge trials, with [passing CI](https://github.com/siamakenna/opportunitybridge-citygov-2026/actions/runs/41228288850). These support a functioning prototype, not 13 users or 209 observations of benefit.

The [classification evidence](CLASSIFICATION_RESULTS.json) supports 12/12 known description-assisted cases and 28/28 authored frozen challenge cases, with 19/28 coverage and nine required abstentions. Normal RSS usually lacks the synthetic descriptions. Frozen author-visible cases are not independently unseen, and no independent adjudicator establishes external accuracy. The [retained error record](BEFORE_AFTER_ERRORS.json) and provenance disclosures are strengths of reporting, not proof of effectiveness.

The [seven-scene demonstration](../../media/v093-walkthrough-transcript.txt) is a 180-second sequence of genuine stills. It makes the story legible; it does not establish continuous usability or participant comprehension. The [evaluation record](../../docs/CAREER_BRIDGE_EVALUATION.md) explicitly leaves speed, comprehension, usefulness, equity and employment outcomes unmeasured.

## Unresolved weaknesses

- One reported informal reviewer; zero verified timed human sessions and zero independent gold adjudicators.
- No committed host, advisor, funding or named staffing/backups.
- Source freshness holds and unverified applicant conditions remain; source retrieval does not renew qualification approval.
- No broad description feed makes synthetic description-assisted coverage representative of production RSS.
- Actual 200% zoom and screen-reader operation remain UNTESTED.
- No equivalent-task human speed/usefulness study or authenticated competitor workflow comparison.
- Sample action order proposes work before a current announcement check; whether users misallocate effort is unknown.
- Dense output may require substantial reading; no observed comprehension result establishes suitability for intended users.

The highest-value competitive evidence would be consenting learners completing the current preparation flow, independent advisors rating the resulting plans, and actual review/support workload under a willing host. These are near-term prototype validations; completed placements are not required to enter or receive credit for a plausible impact pathway.
