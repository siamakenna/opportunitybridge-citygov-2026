# OpportunityBridge: voluntary 15-minute unfamiliar-adult check

Status: prepared, not executed. Independent n=0. Owner sends invitations only after choosing recipients; none have been sent. Seek an adult learner/recent graduate, an undergraduate adult and an advisor/workforce professional. These are suggested perspectives, not a representative sample or committed participants.

Consent (0–2 minutes): Explain purpose, voluntary participation, ability to skip/stop without consequence, fictional data only, no service/grade/employment relationship, and what anonymous notes will be kept. Do not record audio/video or collect contact/private educational records by default. Ask whether the adult consents to observation and anonymous notes; blank or declined means do not proceed. Accommodations and accessible alternatives come first. Owner retains necessary scheduling/contact linkage separately; delete anonymous raw notes within 90 days unless an approved host policy requires otherwise. Never publish identifying quotes without separate permission.

Use the same frozen Maryland Administrative Specialist I source packet and fictional learner for every session. Public app: open the Constellation, choose Coding and data and “Translate public-service evidence.” Controlled software fixture is separately labeled in the test harness; do not mix it with a current availability claim. Fictional learner has 30 accredited credits; all other circumstances are unspecified. The answer key is the frozen Source Detective official snapshot: 30 credits may substitute for six administrative months; education and other conditions remain unresolved. Independent source/HR adjudication is still needed.

2–5 minutes — Discover: Find a relevant correctly classified opportunity; distinguish the administrative job from an internship, and training/directory from an opening. Record actual task start/end, completion, errors, controls/language confusion and assistance. Do not coach the answer.

5–8 minutes — Understand: Locate one actual employer requirement/substitution in the full source. Explain what the fictional learner’s credits do and do not establish. Record exact interpretation and source correctness; do not infer eligibility from preferred skills.

8–11 minutes — Practice: Complete Source Detective and explain feedback. Learner may stop or skip. Record attempts, misunderstanding, assistance and any access barrier; never convert absence of feedback into success.

11–14 minutes — Act: Bring the fictional exercise into Passport. Export a source-linked handoff or Passport, identify an appropriate human next step and an unresolved employer question. Optionally demonstrate saving, reload and deletion only if time permits; no extra requirement to store anything. Delete disposable study progress afterward with consent.

14–15 minutes — Reflection: Ask “What was confusing or unhelpful?”, “How useful was this for this task, from 1 to 5?”, and “Would you choose to return, and why?” Return intention is self-report, not observed retention. Record negative feedback verbatim only with permission and remove identifiers. State the next step and thank the participant.

Optional matched manual-source baseline: A full comparison needs a separately scheduled session or fewer tasks; do not squeeze an unmeasured baseline into this 15 minutes. Use the pre-established equivalent source/profile, counterbalance manual/app order across consenting adults where feasible, record actual time and assistance in both, and preserve incomplete/skipped tasks. Report descriptive observations and exact denominators; no causal claim from a tiny convenience sample. Existing detailed evaluation protocol and blank two-rater forms remain authoritative for a hosted pilot.

Do not fill the receipt until an actual consenting adult participates. Report invited/consented/started/completed denominators separately. No fake users, testimonials, baseline times or institutional commitments.
