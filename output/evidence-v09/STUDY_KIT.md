# Prepared study kit — invitations unsent

## Invitation draft

We are preparing a voluntary 15-minute study of understanding public-service career information using fictional scenarios. Adults aged 18+ unfamiliar with OpportunityBridge may participate through a future authorized host. Declining affects no services or opportunities. Accessible formats and support are available. Any approved compensation is independent of completion. Host/contact/date fields must be completed after governance review. This invitation has not been sent.

## Consent and eligibility

Use the consent/withdrawal template in PARTNER_OPERATIONS.md. Insert the responsible host contact, review determination and retention policy. Explain fictional data and preparation limits, then obtain actual consent before timing. Record only age-18+ confirmation, not birth dates or sensitive eligibility records. Assign a random study code; scheduling contacts remain separately with the host. No actual resumes, records or contact details belong in the app or public results.

## Matched materials and assignment

STUDY_SOURCE_PACKET.json supplies the identical frozen Admin I record, URL, source review date and digest to both conditions. A has 30 credits/zero months; B has zero credits/six months; everything else is unknown. Ask the five protocol questions verbatim in both. Manual gets the source packet and blank paper plan. Assisted gets the same packet plus app tools. Use the protocol's four counterbalanced assignments sequentially before observing results. Source learning and order effects remain limitations. Do not rate whether someone completes every quest in five minutes as a career outcome.

## Observer checklist and timing

Confirm consent, code and assignment. Record device/browser/format, actual start, finish or timeout, interruption seconds, assistance minutes, observed navigation failures, participant answers and proposed plan, unsupported claims, missing facts, negative feedback and access barriers. Stop each condition after five minutes. Record what happened, not desired results. HUMAN_RESULTS.csv has blank actual cells and independent rater columns; no fabricated participants or time data.

## Posttask questions — three-minute reflection

For each condition ask clarity, confidence in understanding and intention to return on 1–5 scales. Ask: What felt confusing or unhelpful? Any access barrier? Did the tool seem to promise eligibility? Which next step would you actually take? Would a human advisor help? Answers are optional; retain unfavorable or missing responses. Enthusiasm does not establish actual career progress.

## Independent adjudicator worksheet

Score each of the five questions 0/1 with source passage and explanation. Rate actionability 0–5: source, valid condition or role, proposed artifact, human question, limits. Count unsupported claims and missing conditions separately. Reviewer 1 and reviewer 2 work independently without seeing each other's answers. Record confidence rationale, notes and an exclude/invalid option. Preserve both raw reviews. A third adjudicator records disagreements and reconciliation with reasons. Do not publish agreement statistics at n=0. Evidence Room downloads a source-only benchmark packet and local reviewer decision files; nothing is transmitted.
