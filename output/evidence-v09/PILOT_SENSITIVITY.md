# Eight planning sensitivities — not observed outcomes

PILOT_SCENARIOS.csv/JSON calculates every scenario from one model. Base: 112 hours/$6,240 economic/$500 cash. Best: 95 hours/$5,357.50 assumes fewer changes and faster reviews, not evidence. Stress: 207.15 hours/$11,531.50 with 37 pending versions stops the pilot.

1. Double review time, 0.4→0.8 hours per reviewer: 141.6 hours/$7,646; 19-version backlog at current capacity. Add 29.6 review hours ($1,406 economic cost), reduce scope or hold records. Extra donated time is not guaranteed even if modeled cash stays $500.
2. Double participant support, 0.75→1.5 hours: 130.75 hours/$7,177.50; adds 18.75 advisor hours/$937.50. Secure coverage before increasing load.
3. One reviewer unavailable: all 37 pending source approvals held. Required role budget stays 112 hours/$6,240; omitting review does not make the pilot viable. Base HR replacement needs 16 hours×$60=$960 additional cash if the rest is donated, total cash $1,460. Replacement is already in economic labor cost and is not counted twice.
4. Half recruitment, rounded to 12 adults: 90.42 hours/$4,945.20; cash $305. Fixed overhead remains. Modeled 9.6 plans is an expectation, not a person count or equity finding.
5. Reserve $125→$625: 112 hours/$6,740; cash $1,000. Host validates actual accommodation needs; this reserve is not an access cap.
6. Five changed/expired versions per week: 55 source-version events; 144.4 hours/$8,004; 18-version backlog. Add 14.4 review hours and 18 maintenance hours, or reduce scope. Changes never auto-approve mappings.
7. Follow-through 0.6→0.3: cost unchanged; expected follow-through 15→7.5. There is no effectiveness observation; functional software may provide little value.
8. Donated labor lost: cash $500→$6,240. Secure funding or reduce scope before launch; free static hosting does not remove human cost.

Stop above five pending versions or below two independent reviewers. Resume only after coverage, source reapproval and host authorization. The model keeps the 60-day proposal; two curation weeks and editable activeWeeks determine workload. Unit costs divide economic cost by assumed recruits/plans; they are not observed cost-effectiveness.
