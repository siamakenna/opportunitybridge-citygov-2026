## Source reviewer SOP

1. Steward records exact recruitment/URL, date, permitted source digest and full conditions. Unreachable, redirected, missing or withdrawn sources are held; observation cannot approve them.
2. Two named reviewers separately read the complete official source. Each records identity, passages, mapping, materials, deadline, pay, remaining unknowns and actual minutes. Do not read the other review first.
3. Keep both raw decisions and disagreement. A third adjudicator resolves with source and rationale; otherwise interpretation remains unknown. Never average decisions into eligibility certification.
4. Maintainer runs snapshot/tests and uses the existing approval process only after recorded host approval. Preserve prior snapshot, version and reason. Recheck after change and before use; active interpretation expires after 48 hours.
5. Steward logs review minutes, queue and expiry weekly. Metadata observations never reset approval age. Missing reviewer coverage stops approvals until a replacement is ready.
