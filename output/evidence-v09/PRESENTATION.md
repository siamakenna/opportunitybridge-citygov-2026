## Three-minute Q&A preparation

Handshake? Discovery platforms find opportunities. This prototype makes selected source interpretation, learning and unverified evidence planning inspectable. Superior learner outcomes are unmeasured.

Why not ChatGPT? Open-ended generation can help. This workflow binds narrow rules to approved versions and exposes unknowns. It has not been shown superior to all AI advice.

Why deterministic? Reproducibility and narrow reviewability, at the cost of smaller coverage: type6/12 makes that tradeoff visible.

What prevents fabricated eligibility? No full certification, source holds, limited claim rules and human questions. Literal matching cannot establish truth.

Source changes? Hold interpretation; observations never renew approval. Independently double-review before resuming.

Beyond Maryland? Architecture can extend, but new sources need terms, access, local expertise and new evaluation. No current national coverage claim.

Why this budget? It is a workload model, not observed cost. Doubling review time creates19backlogged versions and a stop at current capacity.

Career impact? Later consented applications/interviews with appropriate comparison. First study comprehension/actionability, not hiring prediction.

What if studies show no benefit? Publish negative findings, stop or simplify. Functional software alone is not learner value.

Why municipal trust? Inspect sources, versions, failures and limits. Employer decisions remain with employers.

Human effort? Base37source versions ×2reviewers ×24minutes=29.6review hours, before adjudication/monitoring. Coverage is fragile.

What is independent? Official publisher documents are external. Benchmark labels, participant benefit and judging assessments are not independent today; n=0.
