# Proposed 60-day pilot feasibility

Host Status: Prospective partner — no commitment obtained. No funding, staff, recruits or independent outcomes. Base assumptions retain 25 consenting adults, 25 initial opportunities, 112 hours/$5,740 labor plus $375 compensation and $125 reserve = $6,240 economic cost. Cash $500 requires all labor donated. Rates are illustrative loaded planning values, not quotes.

Edit the browser calculator at evidence.html#reality or the JSON assumptions. `node scripts/pilot-model.mjs path/to/assumptions.json` calculates base/best/stress and eight sensitivities. CSV assumptions/results and the calculation module are provided. No participant value has been measured.

Base source workload: 25 initial versions plus two changed versions/week over six active weeks =37 events. Two independent reviewers spend 0.4 hours each per event: 29.6 review hours. HR adds 1.2 adjudication hours, steward 5.2 monitoring hours: 16 hours×$60 and 20 hours×$35. Campus: seven fixed hours plus 25×(0.75 support +0.25 follow-up) =32 hours×$50. Technical: eight fixed hours plus 12 changed versions×one hour =20 hours×$60. Evaluation: 3.5 fixed plus 25×0.5 =16 hours×$50. Access: four fixed plus 25×0.16 =eight hours×$60. Total 112 hours/$5,740. Adjudication and monitoring allowances are assumptions to replace with actual logs.

Source-review capacity is ten hours in each of two curation weeks and 1.6 hours in each of six active weeks: 29.6 total. Base has no end backlog. Fractional weekly source arrivals are workload averages, not half records. The model recalculates capacity/backlog, role hours, participant workload and costs. Assumed 80% completion means 20 modeled plans: $312/model plan; $249.60/recruited adult. These are modeled unit costs, not observed cost-effectiveness.
