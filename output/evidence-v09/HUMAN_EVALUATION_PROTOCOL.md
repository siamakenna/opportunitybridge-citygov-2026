# Locally frozen 15-minute study protocol

Independent participant evidence: n=0. Invitations sent: zero. Independent raters: zero. This public protocol will be frozen in Git before outcomes; it is a preregistration-style record, not an external registry registration. Record any amendment with date and reason before collecting data. A host must obtain its governance/research determination before recruitment; no ethics approval is claimed.

Recruit consenting adults aged 18+ who are unfamiliar with the app. Use fictional facts only. Accessibility needs should lead to equivalent support, not automatic exclusion. Compensation, if approved, is independent of completion; declining or withdrawing never affects services, grades or applications. Host/contact and retention terms must be completed before consent.

Research questions: Can a learner identify an official opportunity, interpret a substitution, recognize missing evidence, propose a truthful plan and distinguish preparation from eligibility? Does the learner find the process clear or useful? None of these directly establishes career impact.

## Equivalent conditions and actual timing

Use the same frozen Administrative Specialist I record, official link, source receipt, materials and five questions in both conditions. STUDY_SOURCE_PACKET.json supplies these. Manual/source-only gets the record and blank plan. Assisted gets the same packet plus OpportunityBridge, optional Source Detective and Evidence Passport tools. Both have the same five-minute cap. A whole quest may not fit; incompletion is a valid observation. Do not give one condition easier information or coach only the assisted condition.

Schedule: two minutes consent/instructions, five minutes condition one, five minutes condition two, three minutes reflection. Observer records actual start and finish or timeout. No fabricated manual time or comparison with browser-script runtime. Support interruptions are recorded separately.

Counterbalance four assignments: manual A then assisted B; assisted A then manual B; manual B then assisted A; assisted B then manual A. Assign sequentially before outcomes. The same source creates learning/carryover, which must be reported. With two participants, present individual paired observations and limits, without significance, population efficacy or subgroup-equity claims.

Profile A: fictional 30 accredited college credits and zero qualifying administrative months. Profile B: fictional zero credits and six qualifying months. Other education, authorization, equivalence and evidence facts stay unknown. Neither profile contains actual participant career information.

## Frozen key and measures

The five questions are: identify the official source/type; interpret the selected experience condition; identify missing full conditions/evidence; propose a source-linked artifact and human question; explain preparation versus eligibility. Authored key: official Maryland Admin I, full-time vacancy; both declared selected routes appear consistent conditional on full context and records; education/authorization/remaining conditions are unknown; artifact is proposed, not completed professional work; employer/human verification is still required. This key awaits independent review.

Record each question 0/1, completion/timeout, unsupported claims, missing requirements, actual elapsed time, navigation failures, assistance minutes, advisor actionability 0–5, clarity/confidence/intention to return 1–5, negative reactions and access barriers. Actionability gives one point each for source, valid target, proposed artifact, human question and retained limits. Keep self-reported confidence separate from advisor ratings. Two raters work independently; keep raw disagreements and a third adjudicator's separate reconciliation. Agreement remains pending until genuine reviews exist.

Use HUMAN_RESULTS.csv, which contains headers and no outcome rows. `node scripts/analyze-study.mjs output/evidence-v09/HUMAN_RESULTS.csv` returns pending n=0 for blank input. With actual consented rows it reports individual records and timestamp elapsed time; a human must audit consent, ratings and missing observations. Preserve noncompletion and unfavorable findings. Exclude withdrawn data according to disclosed policy, never silently drop difficult tasks. Later career impact needs separately consented actual follow-up and an appropriate comparison.
