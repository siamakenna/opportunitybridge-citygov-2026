# Proposed 60-day schedule, budget and adoption assumptions

No host, labor or funding is committed. Go/no-go requires approved roles, capacity, source-gold review, consent and accessibility support.

| Week | Work | Accountable functions | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Campus/city co-design, governance and owner/backup assignment | Campus lead + HR | Host agreement and capacity |
| 2 | Curate 25 sources, double-review complete conditions; freeze task packets | HR + content steward | Zero unresolved mapping ambiguity |
| 3 | Consent/recruitment, accessible room/device/paper preparation | Campus + access reviewer | Voluntary adults; no service penalty |
| 4 | First sessions; audit missing work and source transitions | Evaluator + maintainer | Repair blocking barriers before more sessions |
| 5 | Continue counterbalanced tasks; measure actual advisor workload | Campus + evaluator | Keep unfavorable findings |
| 6 | Complete sessions; optional follow-up only with permission | Evaluator | Report starters/completers/withdrawals |
| 7 | Two-rater scoring and 14-day follow-through | HR + evaluator | Separate self-report from confirmation |
| 8–day 60 | Report findings, incidents, costs and adoption decision | All owners | Continue, change or stop |

Illustrative budget: campus 32 h×$50=$1,600; HR 16 h×$60=$960; steward 20 h×$35=$700; maintainer 20 h×$60=$1,200; evaluator 16 h×$50=$800; accessibility 8 h×$60=$480. Total 112 h/$5,740 labor. Optional compensation 25×$15=$375 and materials/access reserve$125; total economic estimate$6,240. Cash$500 only if all labor is contributed. Validate rates, overhead and accommodation needs with hosts; no expenditure authorized.

Track ≥90% criterion/source accuracy and zero false definite eligibility statements; ≥80% task starters with actionable plans scoring 4/5; ≥60% consenting follow-up learners reporting their next action within 14 days; zero unresolved blocking barriers and median advisor review≤15 measured minutes. All are targets. Denominators, missing data, assistance and unfavorable results must be reported. A small pilot cannot establish subgroup parity or causal hiring/speed benefits.

Adoption assumptions: a named campus advisor, municipal reviewer, steward and backup maintain source freshness; continued hosting stays static and account-free. Scaling requires explicit staff coverage, accommodation/translation resources and institution-approved data retention. Source volatility, source-review workload and participant access are costs, not solved by free hosting. Stop if source reliability, privacy or access cannot be maintained. Delete contact linkage/raw feedback within 90 days unless approved policy requires otherwise; keep only consented de-identified summaries.
