# Advisor onboarding checklist — proposed pilot

No institution is committed. Before recruiting, name a campus lead and backup; confirm capacity, institutional approvals, rates and accessible facilities.

- [ ] Read full source conditions and distinguish employer eligibility from selected app interpretations.
- [ ] Complete the MPT fictional guided tour, edit the worksheet, download and read an advisor handoff.
- [ ] Explain voluntary adult consent, withdrawals, contact/retention policy and equal access to ordinary services.
- [ ] Assign pseudonymous IDs; store scheduling/consent linkage separately in approved host systems.
- [ ] Offer paper/telephone support, devices and requested accommodations; test actual assistive technology.
- [ ] Independently inspect the frozen manual/assisted source packets. Never supply an answer in just one condition.
- [ ] Measure actual review minutes; preserve missing work, assistance and negative findings.
- [ ] Escalate unclear qualifications to municipal HR; disable the mapping rather than improvise equivalence.
- [ ] Verify 14-day follow-up permission; distinguish self-report from advisor confirmation.

The public app uses fictional information only. Never upload real résumés, patient, student or job-seeker records to this demonstration. No application or message is sent automatically.
