Can a learned skill become a credible next action?

For adult learners, graduates and career changers who need help explaining how coursework relates to public-service work. OpportunityBridge turns a fictional skill declaration and a real employer source into a portable preparation plan. It does not certify qualifications, send applications or promise employment.

Verify the outcome in five minutes

  1. Start (30 seconds). Open the HTML example. No account, payment, personal records or quest completion is required.
  2. Inspect the connection (60 seconds). Read the labels: learner supplied (fictional), employer source says (reviewed summary), app explains/suggests, and still unknown. MPT’s reviewed educational-web duty is distinct from the proposed library-page exercise.
  3. Check the source (45 seconds). Follow the official announcement link. Inspect its recruitment identity, review date and hold. Enrollment or accepted recent-graduate status, schedule and authorization remain unresolved. An old review is not current employer approval.
  4. Leave with a plan (45 seconds). Read exactly three next actions. For held sources, check the current announcement first. Download Markdown; inspect the actual source URL, date, fictional status, questions and preparation artifacts. Sharing is voluntary.
  5. Challenge the evidence (60 seconds). Open the Evidence Room. Historical CCEB results remain 23/25 conditions, 10/10 deadlines and 6/12 types versus simpler baselines’ 12/12. New description-assisted 12/12 and frozen authored 28/28 results are bounded software evidence, not independent production accuracy.
  6. Check transfer and access (60 seconds). Try the spreadsheet example. Its proposed fictional vendor-record quality memo maps to a different reviewed employer duty. Financial-experience and credit substitutions require HR verification. Passed deadlines become historical practice and start with a current alternative. Repeat the route on a narrow screen.

What is demonstrated, and what is proposed?

Working software produces three source-linked actions, readable/structured exports, explicit unknowns, optional device saving and safe imports. Three Career Quests, Evidence Passport, Advisor Studio and alternative Constellation routes remain available.

Human evidence: one owner-reported informal qualitative reviewer; zero verified formal timed participant sessions; zero independent benchmark adjudicators. Twelve earlier AI engineering rehearsals are not people. No measured placement, equity or unaided-comprehension advantage is claimed.

Access: keyboard, semantic controls, focus, status and narrow-screen reflow have software receipts. Actual 200% browser zoom and VoiceOver require native permissions; see the dated test record for the current result. No WCAG certification. English-only and cognitive-load limitations remain.

Pilot: proposed five-adult feasibility stage, five double-reviewed sources and one willing advisor. 24.25 staff hours and $1,415 economic/paid-labor cash cost; $175 cash only if all modeled labor is donated. No host, advisor, funding or participants are committed. Stage two conditionally expands to 25 adults and 25 sources over 60 days, with 112 staff hours and $6,240 economic cost; cash is $500 only if all modeled labor is donated. Measured source-maintenance observations do not establish human staffing rates.
