Inspect the product in 90 seconds.

  1. Open Pulse: distinguish a discovery lead from a preparation-ready source and inspect its dated receipt.
  2. Build an Evidence Passport: connect fictional experience to a source target, inspect a map node/text equivalent, and add a proposed exercise from an attributed story.
  3. Open Advisor Studio, retain insufficient information, download/read the actual JSON or Markdown handoff, and reset. Try the administrative coursework journey too.
  4. Open “Real People, Real Civic Work.” Distinguish NIH’s institutional deliverable report from a former fellow’s own account.
  5. Check the expired intake and optional discovery routes. Practice Mode is clearly fictional and secondary.

Discovery and reviewed sources differ.

Pulse initially normalized 320 valid public Maryland RSS records. These are dated discovery metadata, not individually reviewed or guaranteed open jobs. Three evidence levels and the change queue keep that boundary visible.

Twelve records: five specific Maryland recruitments, two research intakes, four ongoing pathways and one expired intake. Eleven appear by default at observation. Five have deadlines beyond November 6 or are open/continuous; that does not guarantee future availability. Sources age visibly after 48 hours; expired deadlines leave default results.
