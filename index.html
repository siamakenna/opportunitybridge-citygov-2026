WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Career guidance with receipts.

Generic job matching tells you which roles look similar. OpportunityBridge explains what is supported, what is missing, and what to verify next.

◎

Evidence, not eligibility guesses

Each requirement is tied to its precise example excerpt. A missing fact stays unknown instead of silently becoming “no.”

↗

Translate real skills

Research, mentoring and communication become credible examples that a civic employer can actually evaluate.

◈

Equity built into the workflow

No personal referrals, résumé uploads, accounts or paywalls required to build a plan. Human advising remains an option—not an assumed privilege.

PARTNERSHIP MODEL

Small enough to pilot.
Useful enough to scale.

Designed for a partnership between a campus career-services office and a city or county workforce team. No institutional partnership has been established.

Weeks 1–2Co-design with students and advising staff; curate 25 verified civic opportunities.
Weeks 3–6Recruit 25 consenting learners; run assisted and unassisted pathways with standardized measurement.
Weeks 7–8Review advisor workloads, actionable plans, referrals and application outcomes.
