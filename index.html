EVIDENCE AND METHOD

Uncertainty belongs in the conversation.

Declared evidence appears consistent, possible conflict, and insufficient information describe selected source-grounded criteria. They never certify or reject a person. Sensitive conditions remain questions for the employer.

Practice Mode: fictional learners and pathways preserves the original three-state engine and regression fixtures. Guided evaluation separates internal software checks from independent user evidence. Independent unfamiliar participants: n=0.

Shortlists and notes disappear on reload or “Clear this session.” Downloads stay on your device; delete or share them yourself. Hosting receives ordinary page-request logs. External sites follow their own policies. No résumé uploads, automatic applications or messages.
